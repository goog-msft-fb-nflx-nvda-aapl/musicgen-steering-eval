Does concept steering in MusicGen survive a fair evaluation?

Course project, CommE5070 (Deep Learning for Music Analysis and Generation, NTU). We add a learned direction inside a frozen MusicGen so the generated music is pulled toward a target note, then test whether that holds up under paired, significance-tested evaluation. Status: mid-progress. We welcome people who want to join or extend this.

  • The ordering holds on MusicGen medium and under a second, independent evaluator (a frozen MERT note probe).
  • A random direction leaves the hit rate at baseline (0.067 to 0.115).
  • Probe score does not predict which layers steer best; the last 8 layers match a per-note causal search.
  • Not yet shown: no listening test, one concept family (root notes), one model family. Hit rate here is measured by automatic evaluators, so judge by ear with the clips above.
