# Coverage of the TA's research questions (status 2026-10-27)

Source of the questions: the TA's topic description (original Chinese text in `CommE5070_FP_era_market_cover_survey_prompt_v3_gaps.md`). Each row names the experiments that address it, where the numbers are (WORKLOG section or result file), and what remains. Only measured items are listed as done; proxies are named as proxies. Survey guidance: `SURVEY3.md`.

## Open items ranked by value for a paper
1. Human blind listening test (era judgment, melody retention, quality): the main missing evidence for the generation half. Needs participants (teammates).
2. (done) Discogs-VI metadata statistics; remaining: none without ids for PA1.
3. (done) Discogs-VINet identity metric.
4. Second era-conditioned generator or conditioning route (e.g. example-conditioned, ACE-Step 1.5 cover mode): only after checking licenses and weights.
5. Artist-grouped or clique-grouped evaluation: impossible with PA1 (no ids); state as limitation.
