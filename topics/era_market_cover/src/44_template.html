Which excerpt sounds more like the decade?

CommE5070 final project, NTU. 24 pairs of 12-second instrumental excerpts, about 10 minutes. Headphones recommended.

Each pair has excerpts A and B. Listen as often as you like, then choose the one that sounds more like the named decade. There are no right or wrong answers, and the instrumentals are generated by a model.

Data use. We record your student ID together with your answers, only to avoid duplicate submissions and to contact you if you ask. The data stays with the project team, is not published with your ID, and is used only for this course project. You can ask us to delete your answers at any time.
