# Era and release-market cues, and era-targeted cover generation

Topic 2 of the repository (topic 1 is in `../musicgen_steering/`). Topic offered by the course TA as a team-finding example (extension of HW1). People interested are welcome to join.

## Questions
1. Which parts of a song (full mix, vocals, accompaniment) let a frozen audio encoder recognize the release decade (dataset A) and the release market (dataset B) on the PA1 Discogs-VI clips?
2. What do the probes rely on: sung language, arrangement, recording and production traits, or dataset bias? (shuffle-and-remix of stems, LEACE/INLP erasure with random controls, signal-statistics and codec/DSP controls)
3. Can stable era cues control a generated cover that keeps the original vocal and melody and regenerates the accompaniment in a target decade? (MusicGen-Melody with era prompts, optional LoRA, three-branch guidance; evaluated with independent encoders, distribution and quality metrics, and content-preservation metrics)

## Where things are
- `WORKLOG.md` every run with its config and measured result (authoritative); `MASTER_PLAN.md` why each component was chosen, ablation registry; `TODO.md` open items; `TA_COVERAGE.md` the TA's questions against the evidence.
- `SURVEY.md`, `SURVEY3.md`, `SURVEY_metrics.md` literature notes with a verified/unverified marker per citation; raw replies in `survey1/`, `survey2/`, `survey3/`.
- `src/` numbered pipeline scripts (separation, embedding, probes, generation, evaluation, analysis); `src/99_make_all_tables.sh` regenerates every stored-results table from `results/` without a GPU.
- `results/` result files and summary tables (`.npz` embeddings and audio are not included).
- `repro/environment.txt` library versions and hashes; `repro/RUNS.md` index of scripts and outputs (generated by `src/90_make_runs_index.py`).
- Generated demo clips are published in the repository-level `docs/era_market/` (GitHub Pages).

## Reproduce
Re-derive the tables from the stored results (CPU, numpy/scipy):
```
bash src/99_make_all_tables.sh
```
Embedding, generation and evaluation need the PA1 audio (course-licensed, not redistributed), a GPU, and the third-party models listed in `repro/environment.txt`.

## Caveats
Test labels of the PA1 subset are hidden, so accuracies are on the validation split (A n=132, B n=102). Language, genre and market-vs-origin are measured through proxies (Whisper LID, Discogs-EffNet tags). Evaluation is automatic; no listening test has been run. Details and every limitation per experiment are in `WORKLOG.md`.
