To see how much of this is production, we applied era like production chains to the real accompaniment, without generating anything. That moves the twenty tens a lot, plus point two seven three, but does not move the nineteen eighties, minus point oh three six. So the eighties shift is not just a mixing effect, and the twenty tens shift is partly one. A second generator, MuseControlLite, keeps the song almost perfectly, but barely changes the era. Text only MusicGen, without the melody, loses the song, with identification down to point one eight eight.
