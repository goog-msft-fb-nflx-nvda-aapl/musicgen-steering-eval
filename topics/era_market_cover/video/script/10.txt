Some limits. We use twenty four songs and three seeds. The manifest has no artist ids, so we cannot give artist grouped confidence intervals. The evaluators are probes trained on real recordings. Discogs EffNet and the K A D margin are independent checks, and the production encoder Fx Encoder plus plus shows only small effects. Decade labels are release years, not production years. The audio quality scores are mixed. And there is no human listening yet, although a twenty four pair listening test is already built.
