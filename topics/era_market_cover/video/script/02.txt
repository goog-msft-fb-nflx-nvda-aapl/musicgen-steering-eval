The data come from the course Discogs VI split. Dataset A has six release decades. Dataset B has six release markets, all from the nineteen eighties. We separate every clip into vocal and accompaniment with Demucs, embed each stem with two frozen encoders, MERT and MuQ, and train a linear probe per layer, with the layer chosen by cross validation on the training set only. In phase two we keep the original vocal, and let MusicGen Melody regenerate the accompaniment from the original melody and a text prompt naming an era, with three seeds on twenty four validation songs. The main evaluation is quantitative. Listening only comes at the end.
