Setup

Data, stems and what we measure

  • Dataset A: six release decades, 1960s to 2010s.
  • Dataset B: six release markets, all from the 1980s: US, UK, Brazil, Spain, Germany, Italy.
  • Clips come from the course Discogs-VI split; vocal and accompaniment are separated with Demucs.
  • Frozen audio encoders, MERT and MuQ, then a per-layer linear probe, chosen by cross-validation on training data only.
  • Phase 1: probe decade or market from mix, vocals, accompaniment and the recombined stems.
  • Phase 2: MusicGen-Melody, melody from the original, text prompt for an era, three seeds, twenty four validation songs.
  • Main evaluation is quantitative; listening comes last.
