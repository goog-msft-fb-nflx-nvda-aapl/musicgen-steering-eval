Limits

  • Twenty four songs, three seeds; no artist ids in the manifest, so no artist-grouped confidence intervals.
  • The generation evaluators are probes trained on real recordings and partly share encoders with the analysis; Discogs-EffNet, KAD and Fx-Encoder++ are independent checks, and Fx-Encoder++ shows only small effects.
  • Decade labels are release years, not production years; the 1960s prompt fails.
  • Quality: Audiobox PQ drops with MuseControlLite; text-only MusicGen scores higher than the melody-conditioned remix.
  • No human listening yet. A 24-pair listening test is built but not run.
