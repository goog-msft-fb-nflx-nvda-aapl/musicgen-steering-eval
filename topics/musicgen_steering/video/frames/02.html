Topic and research questions

Steering adds a learned direction to MusicGen’s residual stream at inference time so a generated clip is pulled toward a concept, here a musical note. The model stays frozen.

Q1

Does steering hold up?

Paired, significance-tested, checked by evaluators other than the steering probe.

Q2

Which direction method?

RFM, mean-difference, logistic regression and LDA, compared at matched audio fidelity.

Q3

Which layers?

Are the most decodable layers the ones where steering works best?
