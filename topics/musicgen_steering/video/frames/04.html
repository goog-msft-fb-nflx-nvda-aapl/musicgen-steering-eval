Experimental setup

Model and concept

MusicGen small (24 layers), medium for confirmation. 12 root notes, 20 text prompts, 2 to 4 generation seeds, 480 paired clips per cell.

Four directions

RFM top eigendirection, mean-difference, logistic regression, shrinkage LDA. All unit norm, trained on SynTheory notes.

Two injection arms

const8: top 8 layers, weight 1. MusicRFM-style: all layers, probe-weighted, injected with probability 0.3. Strength eta 0.15 to 0.6 (up to 2.0 on medium).

Evaluation

Chroma argmax, a MERT note probe, CLAP for fidelity. Exact McNemar tests on paired clips, Holm correction, Wilcoxon on per-note values.
